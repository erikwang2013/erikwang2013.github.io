You, Me and Them

📅 2014-11-29 🏷️ 随笔Essays 🏷️ 说说Status Updates 🏷️ 随笔吐槽Essays and Rants 📂 生活手记

There are always some unfair terms in life. 1. The bank’s ATM breaks down and keeps spitting out banknotes; you call the bank and someone comes right away. If your bank card gets swallowed instead, they tell you after your call to come to the bank to pick up the card a week later. Lesson: when their own interests are not involved, there is no need to hurry at all. When their own interests are involved, they are more frantic than a monkey. 2. When you rent a place away from home you usually sign a contract with the landlord. If you keep living there after the contract expires and sometimes pay the rent a few days late, the landlord is quite easygoing. If there is any problem with the room, the landlord deals with it promptly. But when you are about to move out, a pile of problems appears… Lesson: when they can make use of you and have an interest relationship with you, they are there whenever you call; when they can no longer use you, they take a few hard cuts at you. 3. You often see people showing off that they know some richest man, some big shot, some TV host, that they are friends with so-and-so. Then one day when they fall on hard times, they find that nobody pays them the slightest attention. Lesson: real relationships are built on a long-term foundation, not on having a meal together, shaking hands, or taking part in a few events together and calling that knowing someone.

Some Things About Working with Code

📅 2014-11-25 🏷️ 随笔Essays 🏷️ 说说Status Updates 🏷️ 随笔吐槽Essays and Rants 📂 生活手记

I had been using the thinkphp framework for quite a long time, and recently after changing jobs I got familiar with it again, only to find that this framework is actually pretty good to use. From the code’s models and function calls, to data output, static generation and debugging… each kind of data presents a different logic, while at the same time sharing the same thinking. PHP has the ideas of Java, yet you can’t just copy Java’s methods wholesale. Today a colleague kept agonizing over this problem, when really he was overthinking it, mixing two different languages together. The ideas can be similar, but the usage differs. Suddenly it struck me that people’s thinking works no differently: related things get linked together and a conclusion is drawn, yet in the end it loses out to the answer that practice gives. Using a framework isn’t hard, and there’s no special trick to it — sometimes you’re just overthinking and your thinking goes off track. The shortcut to success is the same way: you don’t have to have made it by 30, and you don’t have to get certified, it’s just a matter of how much time has settled and how you understand the world. We can’t change what we fundamentally are, but we can work toward changing in the direction of the future. It’s very simple, everything is easy…

The China Hi-Tech Fair

The China Hi-Tech Fair has been held in Shenzhen for several years now, and I never made the time to go see it — until yesterday, when I finally went out of my way to visit. Hall 1, showing the latest in tech trends and visual technology, was a real shock to the senses. The 3D fitting mirror, the free-form laser engraver, the mobile robots… it felt like life will soon be very convenient. I don’t know how big the trade-off is between the pros and cons of this kind of intelligent technology — the more convenience technology brings us, the more drawbacks it produces too. Next year will be ruled by the internet. With the Internet of Things, the Internet of Vehicles and O2O appearing, it seems only two things will be left in life — the network and electricity. Without them you’d feel empty, helpless, blurry. Gesture recognition technology is maturing again too; perhaps in a few days you’ll be able to see yourself in a real game, as if you were there in person, commanding the entire battlefield.

The Wuzhen Internet Conference

📅 2014-11-19 🏷️ 随笔Essays 🏷️ 说说Status Updates 🏷️ 随笔吐槽Essays and Rants 📂 生活手记

Wuzhen, a thousand-year-old ancient town in Chinese history. A great place for tourism — at first glance it has nothing to do with an internet conference. The historic buildings left behind by thousands of years combined with today’s technology throw up far too many questions. Following those questions, I went to Baidu first. Baidu only gave this simple introduction, and the internet conference was mentioned after it. I was still a bit dizzy: why on earth hold an internet conference in Wuzhen, why not in any one of Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou or Shenzhen? Only later did I find out that Wuzhen was the earliest and fastest place in the country to adopt the internet, and the place where the internet economy is most developed — and of course, the organizers also deliberately wanted the internet conference to be held in a small town. And so the first World Internet Conference began in Wuzhen: no asphalt roads, no glass windows, everything kept as it originally was. A kind of ancient atmosphere set against scan-to-pay in the shops and high-speed wireless wi-fi on the flagstone paths seemed especially interesting. The internet conference will gather company representatives from more than a dozen countries and regions for three days to jointly discuss the development of the world’s internet. And so the first edition began.
